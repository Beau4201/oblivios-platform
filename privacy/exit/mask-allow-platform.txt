# Dedicated advertising and measurement hosts of owners that also run ordinary services (ADR-186).
#
# The classifier files whole domains under one category: google.com, facebook.com, bing.com, android.com, adobe.com and
# scene7.com are all "advertising" or "analytics" in it, and masking them would route sign-in, push, image delivery and
# connectivity checks through Tor. The generator therefore protects every masked-category domain of an owner that also
# runs a core, cdn, social or identity service, unless it is named here. Only hosts whose single purpose is advertising
# or measurement belong in this file. One line each: a domain, a tab, the reason. `prefix:` matches every classifier
# domain that starts with the text. Read by the owner at the review of Task 21; a regression found by the device sweep
# (MSK-002c) moves a host to the denylist.
